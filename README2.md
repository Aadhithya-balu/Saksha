# SAKSHA — AI-Powered Crime Intelligence & Investigation Platform

## Problem Statement

Crime today does not originate from a single place or exist in a single database. Criminal activities can emerge from **physical environments, digital platforms, social media, communication channels, financial activities, documents, and other sources of information**.

Law-enforcement agencies often have access to large amounts of data, but this information can be **fragmented, unstructured, difficult to correlate, and heavily dependent on manual investigation**.

For example, an investigation may involve:

* **CCTV footage** from streets, buildings, transport systems, and public places
* **Social media activity** such as public posts, profiles, interactions, and reported content
* **Online platforms and websites**
* **Digital documents and reports**
* **FIRs and previous crime records**
* **Images, videos, and audio**
* **Vehicle and number-plate information**
* **Call and communication records**, where legally available
* **Financial or transaction-related information**, where legally available
* **Forensic evidence**
* **Location and geographic information**
* **Witness or investigation reports**
* **Information from multiple police stations, departments, or jurisdictions**

The challenge is that these sources are usually treated as separate pieces of information.

A CCTV recording may contain a vehicle connected to a person.
That person may appear in an earlier case.
The same person may be associated with another individual.
A social-media account may provide additional publicly available context.
A location may appear repeatedly across multiple incidents.

Finding these connections manually can take significant time.

### The core problem

> **How can law-enforcement authorities transform large amounts of fragmented physical and digital information into connected intelligence that helps investigators understand incidents, discover relevant relationships, manage evidence, and investigate cases more efficiently?**

---

# Proposed Solution

## SAKSHA

**SAKSHA** is an AI-powered crime intelligence and investigation platform that brings together information from **physical, digital, and investigative sources** and transforms it into a connected view of an investigation.

Instead of simply storing crime records, SAKSHA helps authorities move through:

> **Data → Understanding → Connection → Intelligence → Investigation**

The platform can process information from sources such as:

**CCTV + Social Media + Documents + Crime Records + Digital Evidence + Forensics + Geographic Data + Other Authorized Sources**

and help convert them into structured investigation intelligence.

### How it works

```text
Multiple Information Sources
            ↓
      SAKSHA Platform
            ↓
    AI-assisted Analysis
            ↓
People / Vehicles / Places / Events /
Cases / Evidence / Digital Entities
            ↓
    Connected Intelligence
            ↓
Investigation & Analysis
            ↓
Human Verification
            ↓
Actionable Investigation Information
```

---

## From Isolated Data to Connected Intelligence

SAKSHA connects information that may otherwise remain separated.

For example:

```text
CCTV
  ↓
Vehicle detected
  ↓
Vehicle associated with Person A
  ↓
Person A appears in previous case
  ↓
Related Person B identified
  ↓
Publicly available social-media information
  ↓
Common location discovered
  ↓
Related incidents identified
  ↓
Investigator receives connected case context
```

The purpose is not to automatically declare someone guilty or make legal decisions. Instead, SAKSHA provides **relevant connections and evidence-backed information for authorized personnel to review and verify**.

---

## AI-Assisted Investigation

SAKSHA can use AI technologies such as:

* Computer Vision
* OCR
* NLP
* Named Entity Recognition
* Entity Resolution
* Speech-to-Text
* Semantic Search
* Knowledge Graphs
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Pattern and relationship analysis

These technologies can help transform unstructured information into information that investigators can search, connect and understand.

---

## Social Media & Digital Intelligence

Modern investigations may also require understanding relevant **digital activity**.

Where legally authorized and technically permitted, SAKSHA can be designed to work with information from:

* Public social-media content
* Public profiles
* Online posts
* Websites
* Digital documents
* Images and videos
* Other authorized digital sources

For example:

```text
Public Digital Information
          ↓
       Extraction
          ↓
Entities / Locations / Dates /
Events / Relationships
          ↓
      Entity Matching
          ↓
Connected Case Intelligence
```

SAKSHA should respect applicable laws, platform policies, privacy requirements, access controls, and authorization boundaries when handling such information.

```text
Incident
   ↓
Information Collection
   ↓
AI-Assisted Analysis
   ↓
Entity Identification
   ↓
Case & FIR Information
   ↓
Evidence Management
   ↓
Network Analysis
   ↓
Investigation
   ↓

   ↓
```

Different authorized users can therefore work with the information relevant to their responsibilities.

---

## What SAKSHA Ultimately Provides

Instead of asking investigators to search through thousands of disconnected records, SAKSHA aims to provide a unified view:

> **What happened?**

> **Where did it happen?**

> **Who or what is involved?**

> **What existing information is connected?**

> **What evidence is available?**

> **Are there related incidents or entities?**

> **What information requires investigator verification?**

> **What does the available evidence and record history show?**

---

## Vision

SAKSHA aims to transform law-enforcement investigation from:

> **Fragmented data + manual searching**

into:

> **Connected data + AI-assisted intelligence + human-led investigation**

By connecting information from the **physical world, digital world, investigative records, and forensic evidence**, SAKSHA can provide authorities with a more unified environment for understanding and investigating complex incidents.
